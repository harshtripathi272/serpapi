# Check these by ear

The QA script measures sync, loudness and masking; it can't tell whether a sound feels right.
Listen to `out/final.mp4` at these five points:

1. **0:00–0:01** The message pop as the forward arrives. It should feel like a phone notification,
   not a game sound.
2. **0:20.6** The intro logo: cloth thump with a glass tap, just before the voice says "So we built".
   QA says its low-mid body is 2.1 dB over the bed (it wants 3), so check that it doesn't sound thin.
3. **0:30–0:33** Four key presses while the claim is typed, then the click on "Check this photo".
   The second key is the one QA flags as partly masked by the voice.
4. **0:55.5** The verdict reveal. The voice now waits a second so the hit has its own beat;
   check the gap doesn't feel like dead air.
5. **1:46** The air woosh as the reply sheet slides up, then the tap on हिंदी at 1:48.

Then once end to end at normal volume: the voice should always be clear over the pad.
